---
name: Daily Book financial scope
description: Why the notebook khata must stay independent of billing and business reports.
---

Daily Book combines notes/plans with a separate person-to-person money khata.
The khata records advances, loans and settlements, not expenses, sales or profit.
Do not automatically merge its amounts into business financial ledgers.

**Why:** The user requested both notes/tasks and a separate money khata.
An informal entry may overlap with an existing bill or payment, or concern money
outside shop sales. Automatic integration would double-count collections or
misclassify what a person owes.

**How to apply:** Cash/UPI on a khata entry is descriptive metadata, not payment
processing or an EOD collection. Any future connection to bills, supplier/customer
payments or expense accounting needs explicit scope and reconciliation rules.
